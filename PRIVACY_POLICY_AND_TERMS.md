# Part 1 — Privacy Policy

Co-Heart is a mobile app for **guardians** (parents, family members, and caregivers) supporting **learners** (children and young people aged 13 or older with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) in their care). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

## 1. Who can use Co-Heart

- **Guardians must be 18 or older.** We ask for your date of birth before you can create an account.
- **Learners** may be any age, including children under 13. A learner's profile is **created, managed, and consented to by their guardian**. Learners cannot create an account on their own, and they use the app only through the guardian's account ("learner mode").
- Co-Heart is intended for families in **California, USA**.

By creating a learner profile, you confirm that you are the learner's parent or legal guardian, or are otherwise authorized to consent on their behalf, and you consent to the collection and use of the learner's information described in this policy.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information about guardians (provided by you)
- Full name, email address, and county
- Date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or older)
- Password (securely handled by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text)
- A 6-digit Guardian PIN (stored only in hashed form)
- **Optional:** a *Regional Support Category*, such as your learner's support plan as per California Regional Center services, used to help you find families with similar needs
- Content you create: connection requests, messages to other guardians, community events and RSVPs, discussion posts and replies, and tutorials you create or link

### Information about learners (provided by the guardian)
- First name or full name, date of birth or age, gender, county, and photo
- Likes, dislikes, abilities, languages, and comprehension level
- Schedule (classes and events) and the daily activity checklist
- Tutorials linked to the learner

### Information created when a learner uses the app
- Activity check-offs (**automatically deleted after 7 days**)
- Mood selections
- Friend connections, and messages sent using pre-written phrases and picture-symbol cards (**learners cannot type free text**)

### Reports
- When you report a discussion post or reply, an event, a guardian chat, or a guardian's profile, we record who reported it, who it's about, the reason you chose, and **a copy of the reported content** as it was at that moment (for a chat, the recent messages; for a profile, the name and photo).

### Blocks
- When you block a guardian, we keep a record of who you blocked and when, so the block can be enforced. Only you (in **Connections → Blocked**) and Co-Heart admins can see your list of blocked guardians. The blocked guardian is not sent a notification, but while the block is in place, each of you sees the other's guardian shown as **"Guardian: Blocked"** on learner cards and learner profiles. The record is deleted when you unblock them, or when either account is deleted.

### Information collected automatically
- A **push notification token** for your device, so we can send notifications
- Basic technical information needed to run the service (for example, sign-in sessions and error information)

### What we do *not* collect
- We do **not** use advertising trackers or sell data for advertising.
- We do **not** collect your precise GPS location.
- We do **not** collect diagnoses, medical records, or health information. The optional Regional Support Category is a general service category, not a diagnosis.
- If you use **Face ID / Touch ID**, your device handles it. We never receive your biometric data.

## 3. How we use information

We use information only to provide and improve Co-Heart, including to:
- Create and secure accounts, and verify your email
- Show your learner their schedule, activities, tutorials, and friends
- Let guardians find and connect with other families (for example, by county or Regional Support Category)
- Generate tutorials you request using AI (see Section 5)
- Send notifications (for example, tutorial approvals, friend requests, and messages)
- Keep the community safe: profanity filtering, blocking and reporting, and admin review of shared tutorials
- Respond to support requests and comply with the law

We do **not** use learner information for advertising, and we do **not** sell or "share" (as defined by California law) personal information.

## 4. Who can see your information

- **Other guardians** see a limited preview (photo, name, county, and Regional Support Category tags) before connecting, and more once you're connected.
- **Learner friends** and their guardians can see a short public summary of your learner's profile.
- **Private tutorials** (including YouTube videos you link) are not shown to other families in the app.
- **Shared tutorials** you choose to publish are reviewed by Co-Heart admins, then visible to all guardians.
- **Co-Heart admins** can access information when needed to review tutorials, handle reports, and provide support.
- **Reports** are seen only by Co-Heart admins. The person who is reported is not told who reported them.

## 5. Service providers we use

We share information with service providers only as needed to run Co-Heart:

| Provider | Purpose | Information involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Database, sign-in, file storage, and server functions (hosted in the United States) | All account and app data |
| **Anthropic** (Claude) | Writing AI tutorial scripts | The tutorial details you enter (skill, action, comprehension level, language) |
| **OpenAI** | Generating tutorial images | Image descriptions for each tutorial slide |
| **Google** (Cloud Text-to-Speech) | Creating narration audio for AI tutorials | Tutorial narration text only |
| **Google** (Gmail) | Sending account emails such as verification and password reset codes | Your email address and the email content |
| **YouTube** (Google) | Playing YouTube videos you link | YouTube may collect data and **may show ads** under its own policies when a video plays. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced (no-cookie) mode |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the service (with notice to you).

## 6. How long we keep information

- **Activity check-off history:** 7 days, then deleted automatically
- **Account, learner, and content data:** kept while your account is active
- **After you delete your account:** we delete your account, your learner's profile, and associated data. Removal from all systems may take up to [30] days.
- **Reports:** content you report, and content reported about you, is kept for moderation even if it is later edited or deleted, and even if either account is deleted. Reports are kept for as long as needed to review them and keep the community safe.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Review and update:** edit your account and your learner's profile in the app at any time.
- **Delete:** go to **Account → Delete account** in guardian mode. This permanently deletes your account and your learner's profile.
- **Notifications:** turn notifications on or off in the app or in your device settings.
- **Requests:** email **coheartsupport@gmail.com** to access, correct, or delete information, or with any privacy question. We may need to verify your identity first.

**California residents.** Depending on our size and activities, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) may give you the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We honor these requests as described above. We do not sell or share personal information.

**Minors (California Business & Professions Code § 22581).** If a learner under 18 posted content on Co-Heart, their guardian can ask us to remove it by emailing us.

**Do Not Track.** We do not track you across other apps or websites, so we do not respond differently to "Do Not Track" signals.

## 8. Children's privacy

We take learners' privacy seriously. Learner profiles exist only under a guardian's account and with the guardian's consent. Learners cannot type free text in chat, guardian controls are protected by a PIN, and guardians can block or report other users. Guardians can also turn off messaging for their learner at any time with the **Disable Messaging** setting on their dashboard; while it's on, the learner can't open or send messages in any chat. Learners' online status and activity are not shown to other users, and learner mode shows no unread-message counts. A guardian may review, change, or delete their learner's information at any time, or ask us to stop collecting it, by using the app or emailing us. If we learn that we collected a child's information without proper consent, we will delete it.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules that limit who can see each piece of data, hashed PINs, and secure on-device storage for sign-in information. No system is perfectly secure. Please keep your password and PIN private, and contact us right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated.
