# Co-Heart — App Review Notes

**Length:** 3,457 characters (Apple limit: 4,000), leaving ~540 for the demo account details.
**Last updated:** 2026-11-22

Paste the text inside the block below into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes.

- Sign-in **email and password** go in App Store Connect's separate **Sign-In Information** fields (not in these notes).
- Add the **Guardian PIN** to these notes yourself (there is no separate field for it). Login details are intentionally not stored in this file.

**Before submitting, make sure the demo account has:** one learner with schedule items, activities and linked tutorials; ideally a connection with another guardian (to try chat, report and block); a few discussions and events.

```text
ABOUT THE APP
Co-Heart is for guardians (18+) of learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) in California. Only guardians create accounts. Learners use the app through their guardian's account in a simplified, supervised learner mode.

SIGNING IN
Use the demo guardian account provided. After signing in, choose:
- "Mentor & Connect" (guardian mode): protected by a 6-digit Guardian PIN.
- "Grow & Connect" (learner mode): the screen a guardian hands to the learner. Tap the home button and enter the PIN to return to guardian mode.
The demo account has one learner with schedule items, activities and linked tutorials already set up.

WHAT TO TRY
Guardian mode: Tutorials (Search, My Tutorials, Create), Connections, Discussions, Events, and the learner's zone (Friends, Learn, Activities, Schedule).
Learner mode: My Day, Activities checklist, Learn (tutorials), Friends and chat, mood check-in. Speaker icons read content aloud.

AI TUTORIALS
Tutorials > Create builds a short narrated slideshow with AI. Generation takes about 30 to 60 seconds. Private tutorials are ready as soon as they are generated. Tutorials shared with other guardians must be approved by our team first, so a shared tutorial created during review will stay "Pending" until we review it. Guardians preview and can edit every slide before a learner sees it. No learner names, photos or profile details are sent to AI providers.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (GUIDELINE 1.2)
- Terms: before creating an account, users must agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which state zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.
- Filtering: profanity is blocked in chats, discussion posts and replies, and events, both in the app and on our server.
- Reporting: the flag icon reports a discussion post or reply, an event, a guardian chat, or a guardian's profile (on profile sheets and Connections cards).
- Blocking: the block icon on profile sheets, the guardian chat header and Connections cards blocks a guardian. Blocked guardians' content is hidden both ways. Unblock in Connections > Blocked.
- Learner chat: guardians can block, report or delete a learner's chat from the shield icon in the chat header (Guardian PIN required).
- We review reports within 24 hours, remove violating content and suspend or remove offending accounts.

CHILD SAFETY AND PRIVACY
- Learner profiles are created and managed only by their guardian.
- Learners cannot type freely in chat. They use pre-written phrases and picture-symbol cards.
- Learner mode shows no online status, last-seen times or unread-message counts.
- Other families never see contact details. Guardians connect first, then chat in the app.
- No ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and no diagnoses or medical records are collected.
- Linked YouTube videos play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and may show YouTube's own ads. Guardians are warned before linking one.
- The app is not intended for the Kids category. It is a guardian tool with a supervised learner mode.

PERMISSIONS
Photos: used only when a guardian chooses a profile picture for themselves or their learner.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Mentor & Connect > Account > Delete Account. This permanently deletes the guardian account and the learner's profile.

NEW SIGN-UPS
Creating a new account sends a 6-digit email verification code, which may land in spam. The demo account avoids this step.

coheartsupport@gmail.com
```
