Design Rationale

What the new palette does

🌅
Sky → Gold gradient
Every header flows from sky blue at the top to warm golden yellow at the bottom — matching the icon's exact colour story and giving each screen a visual anchor to the brand.
Consistent identity
🎨
Functional colour coding
Sky blue signals navigation and social features. Amber/gold signals learning and achievements. Navy anchors chrome. Each zone is scannable at a glance — important for IDD users.
Accessible clarity
☁️
Warm page ground
The page background shifts from cool #EEF8FF (sky-tinted white) to warm #FFF8EE (glow-tinted) depending on the screen's primary colour — the screens feel like they belong to one world.
Tonal cohesion
🔵
Navy stays structural
The deep navy (#1A3248) is reserved for phone bezels, text, and the guardian tile — quiet anchors that keep the bright palette from feeling ungrounded or childish.
Visual hierarchy
✨
Cards glow, not grey
Card backgrounds use sky-border tinting on white (#FFF) so they feel luminous rather than flat. The border colour shifts between screens to stay in the header's hue family.
Depth & warmth
🦯
High contrast preserved
Navy on white and white on the gradient all pass WCAG AA. The sun gradient is decorative — text never sits on mid-gradient without a white or navy backing.
Accessibility first
