# Co-Heart — Frequently Asked Questions

Co-Heart is an app for guardians supporting learners with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD). Guardians manage everything; learners get a simple, safe space for their day, their tutorials, and their friends.

---

## Getting started

### Who is Co-Heart for?
Two people share the app:
- **Guardians** — parents, family members, or caregivers who set up and manage a learner's account.
- **Learners** — the person with IDD, who uses a simplified version with large buttons and no open typing.

### Where can I get the app?
Co-Heart is currently in testing on **iOS only** (iPhone/iPad), through TestFlight. To request an invite, email **reyaansh.bansal@gmail.com** with the subject **"Co-Heart TestFlight"**. Use the email address linked to your Apple ID — that's where the invitation is sent.

### Is there an Android version?
Not yet. Co-Heart is iOS only for now.

### Who can use Co-Heart right now?
Co-Heart is built for families in **California**.

## Accounts & signing in

### How do I create an account?
Guardians sign up in the app. You'll:
1. Agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, then confirm you're 18 or older.
2. Enter your name, email, county, and a password.
3. Choose a **6-digit Guardian PIN**.
4. Enter the 6-digit code we email you to verify your address.

### What are the password rules?
At least **6 characters**, including at least **one uppercase letter**, **one lowercase letter**, and **one number**.

### I didn't get the verification or reset email.
- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Wait a minute or two and use **Resend code**.
- Make sure the email address is spelled correctly.
- Codes expire after a short time, so use the newest one you received.

### I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and type in the 6-digit code we send you. You can then choose a new password.

### Does my learner need their own account?
The guardian creates the learner's profile. From then on, the guardian switches the app into **learner mode** and hands the device over — the learner doesn't need to remember a password.

## Guardian mode, learner mode & the PIN

### How do I switch between guardian and learner mode?
After signing in, you'll see two options:
- **Mentor & Connect** — guardian mode, where you manage everything. This needs your **Guardian PIN**.
- **Grow & Connect** — learner mode, the simplified experience for your learner.

### What is the Guardian PIN for?
The PIN keeps guardian controls away from your learner. You need it to:
- Enter guardian mode.
- Block, report, or delete a chat from inside your learner's chat screen.

### I forgot my Guardian PIN.
On the PIN screen, tap **Forgot PIN?**, enter your account password, and choose a new 6-digit PIN.

## Your learner

### What can I set up for my learner?
Their profile (name, birthday, photo, county, likes, dislikes, abilities, languages, and comprehension level), their weekly **schedule**, their daily **activities**, their **tutorials**, and their **friends**.

### Can my learner see or change their own profile?
No. Learner profiles are fully managed by the guardian.

---

## Schedule & daily activities

### How does the schedule work?
Add **recurring weekly classes** (day, time, and date range) or **one-time events**. You can cancel a single class without removing the whole series. Your learner sees today's schedule on their home screen, color-coded as happening now, coming up, or finished.

### What are daily activities?
A checklist your learner ticks off each day — things like brushing teeth or packing a bag. Pick up to **10** activities from the preset list.

### Can I see whether my learner completed their activities?
Yes. The activities report shows the last **7 days**. Older activity history is deleted automatically.

## Tutorials

### What are tutorials?
Short lessons that teach a skill step by step. Learners watch them as a narrated, auto-advancing slideshow or as a video.

### What kinds of tutorials can I add?
- **AI-generated tutorials** — choose the skill, describe the character and the action, and Co-Heart creates an illustrated slideshow for you.
- **YouTube videos** — link any public YouTube video.
- **Library tutorials** — browse tutorials other guardians have made that our team has approved, and add them for your learner.

### How long are AI-generated tutorials, and what language are they in?
AI-generated tutorials are always in **English** and run about **30–40 seconds**: a few short slides, each with a simple caption and spoken narration.

### Do AI-generated tutorials need to be approved?
Only if you share them. When you create a tutorial, you choose whether to **Share with other guardians**:
- **Not shared (private):** ready to use as soon as it's generated, with no review needed. Private tutorials don't appear in the shared library and aren't shown to other families.
- **Shared (public):** after it's generated, preview it and tap **Submit for Review**. The Co-Heart team checks it **within 48 hours**, before it appears in the shared library for all guardians. You'll get a notification when it's approved or if it needs changes, and you can withdraw it from review at any time.

### Is there a limit on AI-generated tutorials?
Yes, each guardian can create a limited number per month (currently **10**). The count resets at the start of each month.

### Can other families see the YouTube videos I link?
No. Linked YouTube videos are **private**. They don't appear in the shared library and aren't shown to other families.

### Will my learner see ads in YouTube videos?
Co-Heart never shows ads. But YouTube videos are played by YouTube, and YouTube may show ads before or during some videos. We play them in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode to limit tracking. To avoid ads, choose videos from channels that don't run ads, or use AI-generated tutorials, which never have ads.

### Something went wrong generating my tutorial. What should I do?
Email **coheartsupport@gmail.com** and let us know which tutorial it was.

## Friends & chat

### How does my learner make friends?
You manage your learner's friends from two tiles on your dashboard: **Find Friends** to search for other learners and send a friend request, and **Manage Friends** to see friends, accept or decline requests, and manage blocked learners. Before connecting, you can see a short public profile, including the other learner's guardian. If a request is declined, it's simply removed — either family can send a new one later.

### Can my learner type messages freely?
No. To keep chat safe, learners communicate using **pre-written phrases** and **picture-symbol cards** (a communication board), not open typing. A profanity filter also checks every message as an extra safeguard.

### What can I do if someone bothers my learner?
Open your learner's chat with that person and tap the **🛡️ red shield** at the top of the screen. Enter your **6-digit Guardian PIN** (it continues automatically once all 6 digits are entered), then choose an action:
- **🗑️ Delete Chat** — removes the conversation.
- **🚫 Block User** — removes them from your learner's friends list. You can unblock them later from the **Manage Friends** page (Blocked tab).
- **📢 Report User** — sends a report to our team, who will review it **within 24 hours**. They stay your learner's friend unless you also block them.
- **🔒 Block & Delete Chat** — blocks them and removes the conversation.

The PIN keeps these controls away from your learner. If you need help, email **coheartsupport@gmail.com**.

## Blocking a learner

### Can my learner block another learner?
No. Learners can never block anyone. Only a **guardian** can block a learner, on behalf of their own learner.

### How do I block a learner?
From your learner's **chat** with that learner:
1. Open the chat and tap the **🛡️ red shield** at the top of the screen.
2. Enter your **6-digit Guardian PIN**.
3. Tap **🚫 Block User**, add a reason if you'd like, and confirm.

### Why can I only block a learner from the chat?
Chat is the only place where learners interact with each other, so it's where a reason to block someone would come from. Blocking from the chat ties the block to that interaction, so learners aren't blocked without a reason. That's why there's no Block button on learner cards or profiles.

### What happens when I block a learner?
- They're hidden from your learner's friends list and from **Find Friends**.
- Your learner can't chat with them.
- The other family isn't sent a notification.
- Your learner's other friendships aren't affected, and blocking a learner doesn't block their guardian.

### Where can I see or undo a block?
Go to **Manage Friends → Blocked**. You'll see each blocked learner and the reason you gave. Tap **Unblock** to undo it; they reappear in your learner's friends list if they were friends before.

## Connecting with other guardians

### Can I connect with other guardians?
Yes. Use the **Find** tab to search by name, county, or Regional Support Category, send a connection request, and message guardians once you're connected. Before connecting, you'll only see a short preview (photo, name, county, and category tags).

### What else is in the community section?
- **Events** — browse or create community events. Tap **I will attend** to RSVP (tap it again to cancel); events you're attending appear under **Attending**.
- **Discussions** — post topics and reply to other guardians. You can edit or delete your own posts.
- **Resources** — helpful information and support links.

### How do I report something?
Tap the **🚩** on the item, choose a reason (**Offensive**, **Spam**, **Harassment**, or **Sensitive**), and tap **Report**. You can report:
- a discussion **post** or **reply**
- an **event** (open it from the Events list)
- a **guardian chat** (🚩 at the top of the chat)
- a **guardian's profile** (on their profile card or in **Connections**)

Our team reviews every report **within 24 hours**. The person you report is not told who reported them. You can't report your own content, and each item can be reported once — after that the 🚩 is dimmed and shows **Already Reported**. For anything urgent, email **coheartsupport@gmail.com**.

To report someone in your **learner's** chat, use the guardian controls inside that chat (see *What can I do if someone bothers my learner?*).

### How do I block a guardian?
Tap **🚫** next to their name (on their profile card, at the top of your chat, or on their card in **Connections**), then tap **Block**. You won't see each other's posts, events, or profile, and you can't message each other. Your connection and any RSVPs between you are removed. You aren't sent a notification about a block, but on learner cards and profiles, each of you will see the other's guardian shown as **"Guardian: Blocked"**. Your learners' friendship isn't affected.

### How do I unblock someone?
Go to **Connections → Blocked** and tap **Unblock**. You won't be reconnected; send a new request if you'd like to connect again.

## Privacy, safety & your data

### Who can see my learner's information?
Your learner's full profile is visible to you. Other families see only a short public summary when your learners are friends or are considering connecting.

### How long is data kept?
Activity check-off history is kept for **7 days**, then deleted automatically. Other information stays until you change or delete it.

### How do I delete my account?
In guardian mode, go to **Account → Delete account**. This **permanently** deletes your account and your learner's profile, and can't be undone.
